Time Type Dir Δhdg Entry spd (kt) Min spd (kt) Spd ≥80% Spd ≥90% Lost (m / s) Yaw TWA sym

Grey rows = pre-start. Time is seconds relative to the event key time. Entry spd is the mean SOG over the 30 s before the maneuver (kt). Spd ≥80% / ≥90% are seconds to recover that fraction of entry boat-speed (sustained ≥1 s). Lost = VMG-deficit integral over the 30 s post-maneuver window, in meters and the equivalent seconds upwind. Yaw is peak |dHdg/dt| (deg/s) on a 0.5 Hz LPF heading. TWA sym = |pre TWA| − |post TWA| (deg).
