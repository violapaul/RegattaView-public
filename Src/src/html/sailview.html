Peer Gynt Video Player

A 360 video player for sailing race footage, with instrument data overlay when available.

Click/touch and drag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Hold Shift while dragging to roll the view (desktop). Double-tap to reset (touch).

Control Bar

The bottom bar has play/pause, skip ±5s, a scrubbable progress bar, playback speed (1x/1.5x/2x/4x), mute, fullscreen, and a video selector. Videos are 30-minute blocks sorted oldest to newest. The player starts at the first clip of the most recent day and prompts you to continue when each clip ends.
